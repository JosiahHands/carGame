# Car Game

A 3D driving game. Build your car in the garage, then drive it around a small city
with a car park and a stunt park full of ramps and cones. Drift with the handbrake
for points, and switch between day and night.

Your car has a roof machine gun, a rocket pod and a shockwave blast. Buildings,
parked cars, street lamps and trees can all be destroyed: buildings collapse into
rubble you can drive over, and everything is rebuilt once you drive away. There is
also a nitro boost, slow motion, a minimap, a time trial lap through the city and a
photo mode for saving screenshots.

In the garage you can change the body style, paint colour and finish, stripes,
window tint, rims, wheel size, tyre width, ride height, spoiler, exhaust, roof
weapons, headlight colour, underglow, nitro flame colour and number plate. You can
also tune power, top speed, grip, brakes, steering, drift and nitro. Your car is
saved between sessions.

If the game runs slowly, press Esc for the settings: pick a quality preset (Low to
Ultra), or set the resolution, shadows, view distance, effects and a frame rate
limit yourself, and turn on the FPS counter to see the difference.

Runs on macOS, Linux and Windows. It needs [Git](https://git-scm.com) and
[Node.js](https://nodejs.org) 22.12 or newer. On Windows, run the commands in Git Bash.
Installing adds Car Game to your apps: open it from Launchpad or Spotlight on macOS,
the Start Menu or Desktop on Windows, or the app menu on Linux. Press H in the game
to see the controls.

Install:

```bash
curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/JosiahHands/carGame/main/install.sh | bash
```

Update:

```bash
curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/JosiahHands/carGame/main/update.sh | bash
```
